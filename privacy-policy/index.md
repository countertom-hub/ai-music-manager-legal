# Privacy Policy

## 1. Who operates this service

AI Music Manager is currently operated by an individual, rather than a company.

**Operator name:** Tomáš Kučera  
**Privacy contact:** countertom@gmail.com  
**Country:** Czech Republic.

## 2. Purpose of the service

AI Music Manager is a tool for managing the operator's own music projects and associated professional Instagram accounts, initially RENYVO and potentially ERVAYO. It may retrieve account and media information, engagement metrics, and comments to help the operator review audience interactions.

## 3. Data that may be accessed

Depending on permissions granted and enabled features, the service may access Instagram account identifiers and usernames; media identifiers, captions, permalinks and media metadata; comment identifiers, comment text, usernames and timestamps; and account insights. This can include information relating to people who comment on the operator's Instagram posts.

The service uses an Instagram access token to make authorized API requests. Tokens must not be exposed in publicly accessible files or logs.

## 4. How information is used

Instagram data is used to display and review interactions on the operator's own accounts, identify potentially promotional comments for manual review, and support account management. A heuristic classification is not proof of spam or automated activity. The current read-only comment integration does not automatically delete comments or reply to users.

The operator does not intend to sell Instagram personal data or use it for unrelated advertising profiles.

## 5. Legal basis and data subjects' rights

Where GDPR applies, processing may rely on the operator's legitimate interests in managing professional artist accounts, subject to a balancing assessment. Other legal bases may apply to particular operations. Individuals may contact the operator to ask about applicable rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, or complaint to a supervisory authority.

## 6. Storage, service providers and retention

The service is hosted using third-party infrastructure, including Railway for the application and GitHub for source control and these public documents. Instagram data is obtained from Meta's APIs. **The operator must confirm whether comments or other personal data are persistently stored, the precise hosting locations, the retention periods, and the relevant service providers before publishing this policy.** Access tokens are configured as private application secrets, not as content on this site.

## 7. Sharing and international transfers

Data may be processed by hosting and platform providers as needed to run the service. **Details of recipients, processing locations, safeguards and any international transfers must be verified before publication.**

## 8. Security

The application uses access-controlled endpoints for its management interface. The operator intends to limit access to authorized accounts and protect API credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Data deletion

See [Data Deletion Instructions](../data-deletion/) for how to request deletion. Requests will be assessed according to applicable law and the data actually held.

## 10. Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes, including if its operator becomes a company. The last-updated date will be revised when material changes are made.
