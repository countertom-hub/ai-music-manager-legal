## What happens next

The operator will verify the request as reasonably necessary, identify whether the application actually holds the relevant information, and respond in accordance with applicable law. Data that is not stored by the application cannot be deleted from the application's storage. Removing data from AI Music Manager does not delete content hosted independently by Instagram or Meta.

If the application holds data that must be erased, the operator will remove it from applicable systems, subject to lawful retention obligations and technical backup cycles.

**Before publication, confirm the actual deletion workflow, any stored data categories, and a reachable contact address.**

## Revoke Instagram access

The Instagram account owner can review and remove connected application access in Instagram's settings under Apps and Websites (menu names may vary). Revoking access prevents future authorized requests using that connection but does not necessarily erase previously stored data. Contact the operator if deletion of stored data is also required.
