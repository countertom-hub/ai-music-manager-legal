# Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 25 October 2026.

## 1. Send a request

AI Music Manager is operated by **Tomáš Kučera, Czech Republic**. Email [countertom@gmail.com](mailto:countertom@gmail.com) with the subject **AI Music Manager — Data Deletion Request**.

State the relevant Instagram username, the post or interaction concerned if known, and what application-held data you want erased. You may also use this address to request access, correction, restriction or to object to processing. Do not send your Instagram password, an access token, or unnecessary identity documents. No special paid service is required to submit a request.

## 2. How the request is handled

The operator will identify the relevant information and verify the request only as reasonably necessary. The normal response period is without undue delay and within one month of receipt. If a lawful extension or refusal applies, the operator will explain it and the relevant rights of complaint or remedy.

The operator will check the application, relevant project records, any operator-created exports, correspondence and affected backup copies. The response will say whether relevant data were found and what was erased, restricted or retained under an identified exception. Requests are normally handled free of charge, subject to the narrow exceptions permitted by applicable law.

## 3. Current Instagram storage

The current backend reads Instagram information on demand. Its comment module does not maintain a persistent database or object-storage archive of comments or commenters and has no reply or moderation endpoint. If no application copy exists, the operator will confirm this; there is no stored comment row to delete merely because the original comment remains on Instagram.

An export, screenshot or copy made separately by the operator or an authorized client is a separate record and must be considered where relevant. The absence of comment rows does not mean that technical request logs or privacy correspondence never exist.

## 4. Logs, recovery copies and limitations

Railway states that operational log entries expire after 90 days and cannot be individually deleted early. Where a request concerns those records, the operator will explain this limitation, the records involved and any available restriction or provider escalation. This does not remove applicable legal obligations.

Database backup rotation retains seven successful UTC-date copies and four ISO-week copies. Independent encrypted copies on the operator's Windows device and iCloud have no automatic expiry and require manual handling. If an archive contains data that must be erased, the operator must include it in the erasure assessment and remove affected copies where possible, including applicable recently-deleted storage, or document lawful retention and restricted use. Any restoration must account for previously accepted erasure requests before restored data are used again. Routine backup rotation alone is not a promise of immediate erasure.

The operator may retain information where a legal obligation or another applicable exception requires it, such as the minimum record needed for legal claims. The reason and relevant retention criteria will be explained; unnecessary verification material will not be kept merely because it accompanied a request.

## 5. Disconnecting and Instagram content

The account owner can remove the application's access through the relevant Instagram or Meta account settings for connected apps, or ask the operator to disconnect it. Removal of authorization prevents requests that depend on that authorization; it is separate from deleting copies already held.

Deleting data from AI Music Manager does not itself delete an Instagram comment, post, account or data independently held by Meta. Manage the original content through Instagram or Meta's applicable request channels.

This page provides an email-based deletion-instructions procedure. It is not an automated signed-request callback endpoint and does not issue a callback confirmation code.

See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/) and [Terms of Service](../terms/). If you disagree with the handling of your request, you may contact the [Czech Office for Personal Data Protection](https://uoou.gov.cz/) or another competent supervisory authority.
